What drink has the most B12?

Vitamin B12 (cobalamin) is essential for nerve health, red blood cell formation, and energy metabolism. While B12 occurs naturally in animal products, drinkable sources are typically fortified or provided as concentrated supplements. This article summarizes which beverages tend to contain the highest amounts of B12 and what to consider when choosing a B12 drink.

Highest-B12 drink categories

Three beverage types commonly provide the largest doses of vitamin B12:

  • Vegan B12 shots and liquid supplements: Small, concentrated shots often deliver hundreds to thousands of micrograms (mcg) per serving. They use bioavailable forms such as methylcobalamin for faster uptake.
  • B12-fortified energy drinks: Some energy formulas include very high B12 levels, ranging from several hundred to several thousand mcg per can, combined with other stimulants or nutrients.
  • Fortified plant milks and smoothies: While lower in absolute dose than shots or high-potency energy drinks, fortified soy, oat, and almond milks typically supply 1.5–3 mcg per cup (approx. 100–125% of the recommended daily value), making them useful for daily maintenance.

Forms and bioavailability

Two common supplemental forms appear in beverages: cyanocobalamin and methylcobalamin. Methylcobalamin is frequently preferred for neurological support and cellular uptake, while cyanocobalamin offers greater stability in processing and shelf life. Evidence suggests both forms can correct deficiency when delivered at appropriate doses, but individual absorption varies, especially in people with intrinsic factor deficiency or malabsorption.

Practical considerations

High-dose B12 in a single drink (hundreds to thousands of mcg) is generally considered safe because B12 is water-soluble and excess is excreted. However, there are practical factors to weigh:

  • Read labels carefully for dose, form, and other active ingredients such as caffeine or added sugars.
  • For routine intake, fortified plant milks and smoothies provide steady daily coverage; concentrated shots can be useful for targeted supplementation.
  • Timing with meals can improve absorption for some individuals; healthcare guidance is recommended for those with known deficiencies or medical conditions.

Summary

Drinks with the most B12 are typically concentrated vegan shots and certain fortified energy beverages, while fortified plant milks and smoothies provide reliable daily intake suitable for long-term maintenance. Choose products with clear labeling of the B12 form and amount, consider your absorption needs, and consult a healthcare professional if you suspect deficiency.
