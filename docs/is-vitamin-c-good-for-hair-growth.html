Is vitamin C good for hair growth?

Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) plays several biologically plausible roles in supporting hair health: it is an antioxidant, a cofactor for collagen synthesis, and a facilitator of non-heme iron absorption. These functions together help maintain a scalp environment that supports healthy hair growth, although vitamin C alone will not reverse genetic hair loss.

Antioxidant protection is central to vitamin C’s potential benefit for hair. Oxidative stress from UV exposure, pollution, smoking, or chronic inflammation can damage follicular cells and accelerate hair follicle aging. By neutralizing free radicals, vitamin C may help preserve follicular integrity and extend anagen (growth) phases in some contexts, as discussed in dermatological and trichology literature.

Vitamin C is also indispensable for collagen synthesis, an extracellular protein that supports the dermal papilla and surrounding matrix that anchor hair follicles. Without adequate ascorbate, collagen formation is impaired, which can reduce tensile strength and resilience of hair-supporting tissue. Some clinical and experimental studies suggest combined approaches—collagen peptides plus vitamin C—can improve hair fullness and reduce shedding over months, though evidence varies.

Another practical mechanism is enhanced iron absorption. Non-heme iron from plant foods is less bioavailable than heme iron, and vitamin C enhances its intestinal uptake. Because iron deficiency is a common reversible contributor to hair shedding, ensuring sufficient vitamin C intake alongside iron-rich meals can be a simple nutritional strategy to support follicle nourishment.

Delivery options include food sources (citrus, berries, kiwi, peppers, leafy greens), oral supplements, and topical formulations. Recommended intakes for adults are generally 75–90 mg/day, with a tolerable upper intake of 2000 mg/day to avoid gastrointestinal side effects. Individuals with specific deficiencies, absorption issues, or medical conditions should consult a healthcare professional before using high-dose vitamin C.
